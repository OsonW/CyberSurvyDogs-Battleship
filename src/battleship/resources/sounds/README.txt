Sound effects folder
====================

Drop your audio files here using these EXACT names (all lowercase):

    miss.wav        - played when a shot misses (hits water)
    hit.wav         - played when a shot hits a ship
    sunk.wav        - played when a ship is destroyed/sunk
    music.wav       - background music (loops during the menu and gameplay)
    endscreen.wav   - plays once when the game-over / win popup appears

IMPORTANT: files must be .wav, NOT .mp3
